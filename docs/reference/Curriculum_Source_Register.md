## Source locations to verify

- 2013 framework mirror (verify against CDC): https://lataz.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Curriculum-Framework-2013.pdf

These are source leads, not a complete downloaded or digitised curriculum collection.
Before importing, verify document provenance, edition, level, subject, page coverage, and reproduction permission. Public availability does not establish a reuse licence.

## Digitisation rules

Keep 2013 and 2023 curriculum editions separate. Preserve the document's own grade/form labels, topic hierarchy, competence/outcome wording and official codes. Include page references and source URL for every imported topic and competence. Mark missing official codes as absent; do not manufacture them. Validate extracted tables and ordering against the source pages before publishing.

## Prepdesk reference

Publicly described flow: choose curriculum level, subject, topic, subtopic and competence; generate a draft; review and edit; save; export to PDF or Word. Adapt the workflow to Synapse without copying private code or proprietary assets. Public descriptions are not proof of signed-in behaviour.
