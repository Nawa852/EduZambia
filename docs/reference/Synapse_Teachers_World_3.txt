 3. Page One — Home (Today)
   • Today’s lessons — tap any to open its plan; time till each period.
   • Action queue — mastery scores waiting to be entered, submissions awaiting verification,
   review-queue items pending. Nothing gets forgotten; nothing needs a notebook.
   • Quick actions — new plan, new quiz, announce, mark attendance.
   • Pulse card — class average mastery this week, trending up or down, and the one competence
   most needing attention tomorrow.
   • School announcements and parent messages — priority first, quiet by default.


 4. Page Two — Plan (AI Planning)
 4.1 Lesson Plans
   • Cascading curriculum dropdowns: grade → subject → topic → sub-topic → specific competence
   — official CDC data, so the curriculum part can never be wrong.
   • One tap generates the complete MoE-format document: general and specific competences,
   lesson goal, rationale, prior knowledge, references (Zambia Education Curriculum Framework
   2023), learning environment, materials (realistic and low-resource aware — charts, manila
   paper, textbooks), expected standard, and the 5-stage progression table (Introduction →
   Development → Exercise/Assessment → Homework → Conclusion) with Teacher activity /
   Learner activity / Assessment criteria columns.
   • Edit inline; regenerate any single section; version history; duplicate across classes; export
   branded PDF.

 4.2 Schemes of Work
   • Fill Week 1 — the engine continues in syllabus order across the term; mark any week Revision
   or Test; the full term’s MoE-format table generates automatically.

 4.3 Voice-to-Plan
   • Speak the lesson aloud — in English or a local language ("tomorrow I want to teach web
   interactivity, they struggled last week, practical session") — Synapse structures it into the
   official format. Talking is faster than typing.

 4.4 Smart extras
   • Differentiation: the same plan rendered at three support levels (core / supported / advanced)
   for mixed-ability classes.
   • Absent-learner recap: a one-page plain-language summary of each lesson for students who
   were absent — generated automatically, sent to their Planner.
   • Curriculum browser: the whole syllabus as a tap-through map — see what was taught, what’s
   next, and how every lesson connects.

 5. Page Three — Assess (Quizzes, Tests, Exams,
 Marking)
 5.1 Quiz & Test Builder
   • Pick a competence → AI generates a quiz (multiple choice, structured, calculation, short
   essay) with answers and mark allocations; adjust count and difficulty with sliders.
   • Diagrams where needed: LaTeX-rendered mathematics, real music notation, generated
   figures for sciences and technical subjects.
   • Push to the class in one tap — it appears in every learner’s Planner with a due date; parents
   are notified automatically.

 5.2 Exam Generation (ECZ-format)
   • Full exams learned from the real ECZ paper bank: authentic section structure, command
   words, mark distribution and time allowance — with a complete marking guide.
   • Per-student variants: the same exam with randomized numbers so answer sharing is useless.
   • Questions inside a live exam are solution-locked for the whole class until the due date; every
   solution view is logged for the integrity audit.

 5.3 AI Marking & Gradebook
   • Submissions marked against the guide; the teacher sees the AI’s marking with the reasoning
   and overrides in one tap.
   • Gradebook auto-built from every quiz, test and mastery entry — no spreadsheet, ever.
   • Comment writer: per-learner report comments drafted from performance data; the teacher
   edits tone and detail, never starts from a blank page.


 6. Page Four — Class
   • Create class → get a class code; learners join in seconds. Multiple classes, multiple grades —
   one view.
   • Mastery entry in under two minutes: after a lesson, mark each learner mastered / not yet for
   that competence — one screen, big tap targets.
   • Attendance-lite: present / absent in seconds; absence triggers a guardian notification.
   • Class list with guardian-link status: instantly see which learners have a connected parent and
   which don’t — share a join code with the unlinked.
   • Learner snapshots: any learner’s pathway — mastery history, strengths, needs work, app
   activity — one tap from the class list.

 7. Page Five — Insights (Analytics & Adaptation)
   • Mastery heatmap: the whole syllabus for the class — green (mastered), amber (partial), red
   (needs work) — at competence-code level.
   • "Who needs what" list: per learner, the exact competences to revisit — with one-tap revision
   packs generated for each.
   • Adaptive engine: below-threshold class mastery → the next lesson plan auto-opens with
   remediation; strong mastery → enrichment. The class teaches the plan; the plan adapts to the
   class.
   • Trends: week-over-week movement, comparison to the school average, early warning before
   marks slip.
   • Integrity audit: which students viewed solutions before a test, and when — evidence, not
   suspicion.


 8. Page Six — Connect (Communication)
   • Announcements: to one class, several, or the whole school; parents receive them in their own
   feed; read receipts show who has seen it.
   • Class groups: moderated chat with the learners of a class — questions after school, homework
   clarifications — with the teacher in control and everything logged (no more WhatsApp groups
   with no oversight).
   • Study-group visibility: see the school-scoped study groups your learners joined and drop in to
   answer — teaching extends beyond the classroom walls.
   • Parent messaging: direct, logged conversations; canned updates ("your child mastered 4.1.4
   this week") sent in two taps.
   • Meetings: publish available slots; parents book; reminders to both sides. No phone-call tag.


 9. Page Seven — Documents (Branded & Print-Ready)
   • The school’s identity on every page: logo, colors, school name and EMIS header on lesson
   plans, schemes, tests, report cards and certificates — print-ready PDFs that stand up in
   inspections.
   • Report cards: the term’s reports auto-composed from mastery data, per learner, with
   AI-drafted comments the teacher edits in one tap.
   • Certificates: achievement certificates for competitions, perfect scores, improvement —
   generated and printed in seconds.
   • Print center: batch-export anything, formatted for the school’s printer.

 10. Page Eight — Library (Resources & Review Queue)
   • My resources: every plan, scheme, quiz and document the teacher has made — searchable,
   reusable, versioned.
   • School-shared library: colleagues’ plans rated and organized by topic; the school’s collective
   knowledge stays even when a teacher transfers.
   • Upload anything: a past paper, textbook chapter, or notes → auto-digitized and tagged to
   competences. Low-confidence items land in the review queue — one tap confirms or fixes.
   Teachers curate the platform in seconds and the whole system gets smarter.


 11. Page Nine — Grow (Professional Development)
   • Lesson observations: heads of department record observations against the teaching
   standards, linked to the actual lesson plan taught.
   • Standards tracker: the teacher’s growth across the term, with suggested focus areas and
   relevant resources.
   • Micro-PD: short, targeted tips generated from the teacher’s own analytics ("your assessments
   skew recall — here are two question styles for analysis").


 12. Page Ten — Calendar
   • Term planner: schemes, tests, assignments and events on one timeline; drag to reschedule;
   learners and parents get deadline reminders automatically.
   • Exam-countdown views per class; conflict warnings (two tests same day).


 13. Page Eleven — Me
   • Profile, subjects and classes, school branding preview, language preference, notification
   controls, data & privacy settings, offline downloads.
