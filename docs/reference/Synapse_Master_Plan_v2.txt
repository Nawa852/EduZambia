 1. The Vision
 Synapse is the connected learning platform where learners improve, teachers teach instead of
 doing paperwork, parents finally see the truth, and schools run on evidence. It is lightweight in
 feel — simple pages — but the pages are wired together, and that wiring is the product.

 Principles
   • Synapses over pages. Every page reads from and writes to the shared Learner Model. Data
   flows; nobody re-enters anything; each feature makes the others smarter.
   • The curriculum is ground truth. Digitized Zambian syllabi (ZECF 2023, CDC codes like 4.1.4.1).
   Every plan, question, answer, artifact and report cites it.
   • The knowledge base grows itself. Any uploaded paper, textbook or note is auto-digitized,
   tagged to competences, and entered into the bank. New ECZ papers are known the day after
   upload — forever.
   • Study pathways, not study sessions. Learning is continuous: a pathway follows the learner
   across pages, terms and years — resumable anywhere, even offline (referring to notes in
   church, revising on the bus).
   • Schools are brands. Each school gets a branded account: logo and colors on the dashboard,
   lesson plans, schemes of work, tests, report cards and certificates. Documents look like the
   school’s, not generic.
   • Offline-first, language-inclusive. Everything works without signal and syncs later. Explanations
   and audio in English plus major Zambian languages.
   • Privacy by design. Learners are minors: permissions asked first, monitoring opt-in and
   parent-controlled, guardians see only their children, on-device processing for anything
   sensitive.



 2. Architecture — One Brain, Four Worlds
 At the core: the Curriculum Engine (subjects → grades → terms → topics → sub-topics →
 competences with official codes) and the Knowledge Bank (past papers, questions, solutions,
 materials — auto-digitized). Around them, four worlds connected by synapses:

  World             Feeds into Synapse                           Receives from Synapse

  Learner           Study sessions, practice & exam              Adaptive pathway, assignments,
                    results, artifacts, focus time               resources, study packs

  Teacher           Plans, schemes, tests, mastery               Class learner models, review queue,
                    scores, upload verifications                 curriculum & paper bank

  Parent            Guardian link, viewing activity              Verified progress, study reports,
                                                                 alerts, messages, meetings

  School            Structure, branding, announcements,          Aggregate analytics, document
                    enrollment                                   branding, Ministry-ready evidence
 One engine serves Primary, Junior Secondary and Senior Secondary — each level is syllabus data
 in the same tables. Colleges and tertiary join later through the same ingestion pipeline.

 3. The Learner World — in Detail
 Ten connected spaces. Each is useful alone; together they form a study pathway that never loses
 the thread.

 3.1 Home (Today)
   • Auto-built daily plan; streaks and weekly goals; flashcards due today (spaced repetition);
   exam countdowns; weak-topic nudges ("sets troubled you — 10 minutes?"); pick up where you
   left off — any page, any device.
   • Optional mood/energy check-in — Synapse schedules hard topics when the learner says they
   feel sharp.

 3.2 Synapse AI (the engine room)
   • Curriculum-grounded chat with citations ("this is Commerce 4.1.4, ZECF 2023") — ChatEdu
   beaten by being official.
   • Snap & Solve — photograph any problem; symbolic math engine solves, AI explains every
   step (Gauth beaten by reliability).
   • Voice Tutor — talk it through, hear the explanation back; hands-free revision for
   low-bandwidth homes.
   • Local-language explanations — English, Bemba, Nyanja and more; ask in one language, revise
   in another.
   • Artifact generator — "make me a 3D model of the heart", "a projectile simulation", "an
   interactive timeline of the Zambian economy". Sandboxed interactive HTML, saved to Library,
   shared to study groups, tagged to a competence so classmates on that topic find it
   automatically.
   • Modes: Explain · Guide · Solve · Practise · Assess · Create · Organise · Plan. Eight verbs, one
   engine.

 3.3 Library (Notion × NotebookLM)
   • Files, notes, folders; PDF reader with highlight and annotate; document scan (OCR of
   handwritten notes); web clipper; Ask My Library — answers grounded in your own materials
   with citations to the exact page.
   • Auto-generated companions: every note gets a summary, key points, flashcards and a
   mini-quiz (Break It Down).
   • Formula sheets & glossaries per subject and grade, generated from the syllabus and kept
   updated.

 3.4 Practice (My Circle)
   • Five-step loop — Capture → Understand → Practise → Review → Master — with weekly
   completion. Study in loops, not lines.
   • ECZ paper bank — real papers segmented question-by-question, each tagged to a
   competence, with worked solutions and examiner marking points.
   • Paper Mode — open a past paper; tap any question for its worked solution, marking guide and
   an instant mini-quiz on that topic. A static PDF becomes a tutor.
   • Personalized mocks — "I’ve covered algebra and sets" → authentic ECZ-format timed exam
   from real bank questions, fresh per-learner number variants, auto-marked with explanations.
   • Exam Simulator — full papers under real timing; adaptive selection weights your needs-work
   competences.

 3.5 Study Groups
   • School-scoped groups: shared notes and flashcard decks, group quizzes, challenges with
   leaderboards (opt-in), discussion threads.
   • Escalate any question to Synapse AI — the answer returns to the whole group. One member’s
   upload becomes everyone’s study pack.

 3.6 Planner
   • Tasks, daily goals, focus timer; revision plan generator — "ECZ Maths in 21 days" becomes a
   paced daily plan.
   • The planner schedules weak-topic practice and flashcard reviews automatically from the
   Learner Model — it plans around your reality, not just your to-do list.

 3.7 Progress (outcomes)
   • Mastery per subject and competence, strengths and needs-work, term-over-term trends,
   exam-readiness estimate.
   • Career lens: "where does this competence lead?" — biology competences link to nursing,
   maths to engineering, commerce to accounting — motivation through direction.
   • Certificates: milestone certificates (school-branded) for completed topics and mastered
   terms.

 3.8 Learn Anywhere (offline & audio)
   • Offline-first: notes, flashcards, papers and downloaded lessons work with no signal;
   everything syncs when back online.
   • Audio lessons ("revision radio"): any note or topic becomes a spoken summary — revise on
   the bus, in church before service, while cooking.
   • Cross-device pathway: phone at school, parent’s tablet at home — the pathway resumes
   exactly where it stopped.

 3.9 Achievements
   • Badges and milestones (first perfect quiz, 7-day streak, topic mastered), class leaderboards
   (opt-in), term certificates.

 3.10 Me (Profile & Family Link)
   • Profile, joined classes, language preferences, Family Link (guardian connects with the learner
   code), notification controls, data/privacy settings.

 4. The Teacher World — in Detail
 Fourteen connected capabilities — an AI workspace plus a classroom command center. Everything
 a Zambian teacher needs, nothing they have to format themselves.

 4.1 AI Lesson Planning
   • Cascading curriculum dropdowns (grade → subject → topic → sub-topic → competence —
   official data, never free text).
   • Full MoE-format generation: general/specific competences, lesson goal, rationale, prior
   knowledge, references (ZECF 2023), learning environment, low-resource-aware materials,
   expected standard, and the 5-stage progression table (Teacher activity / Learner activity /
   Assessment criteria).
   • Edit anything inline; regenerate any section; version history keeps every draft.

 4.2 Schemes of Work
   • Fill Week 1 — the engine continues in syllabus order across the term; weeks can be marked
   Revision or Test; full MoE table generated.

 4.3 Voice-to-Plan
   • Describe the lesson aloud in English or a local language — Synapse structures it into the
   official format. For teachers faster at speaking than typing.

 4.4 Tests & Exam Generation
   • Quick tests per competence, or full ECZ-format exams learned from the real paper bank —
   authentic structure, command words, mark distribution — with complete marking guides and
   per-student variants.
   • Diagram support: LaTeX-rendered math, real music notation, generated figures for technical
   subjects.

 4.5 Classroom & Scores
   • Create class → class code; learners join in seconds. Mastery entry after each lesson: mastered
   yes/no per learner in under two minutes.
   • Light attendance; class list with guardian-link status (who is connected to parents, who isn’t).

 4.6 Assignments & AI Marking
   • Assign a paper or generated exam → lands in every student’s Planner with due date and
   notification. Submissions AI-marked against the guide; teacher verifies with one-tap overrides.

 4.7 Class Analytics
   • Mastery heatmap per competence; "who needs what" list per learner; trends across the term;
   comparison to the class average — all feeding the adaptive engine.

 4.8 Adaptive Engine
   • Below-threshold mastery → next plan opens with remediation; strong mastery → enrichment.
   The class teaches the plan, and the plan adapts to the class.

 4.9 Branded Documents
   • Every plan, scheme, test, report card and certificate carries the school logo, school colors,
   teacher name and school header — print-ready PDFs that look official for inspectors and the
   Ministry.

 4.10 Teacher Library
   • School-scoped shared repository: reuse colleagues’ plans, contribute your own, rate and
   organize by topic. A school’s collective knowledge stops leaving when a teacher transfers.

 4.11 Report Cards & Comments
   • Term report cards auto-composed from mastery data — school-branded, per learner, with
   AI-drafted teacher comments editable in one tap.

 4.12 Communication
   • Announcements to a class or the whole school; parent messaging with read receipts; parents
   request meetings from teacher-published slots.

 4.13 Review Queue & Professional Growth
   • Uploaded materials with low auto-tagging confidence wait here — one tap confirms or fixes;
   teachers curate the platform in seconds.
   • Lesson observation notes and teaching-standards tracking begin continuous professional
   development inside the workspace.

 4.14 Calendar & Deadlines
   • Term planner: schemes, tests, assignments and events on one calendar; deadline reminders
   to learners and parents automatically.

 5. The Parent World — in Detail
   • Family Link & multi-child: connect with learner codes; support several children in one account,
   switching between them instantly.
   • Progress dashboard: mastery per subject and competence, trends term over term,
   exam-readiness, class-average comparison — the truth, live, not end-of-term surprises.
   • Verified study reports: "Elena studied 32 minutes today — engaged, verified". Session logs
   from the focus system; optional on-device learning confirmation (opt-in, parent-controlled).
   • Alerts that matter: missed assignments, slipping mastery, upcoming tests, absence notes —
   tuned to urgency, not spam.
   • Documents inbox: school-branded report cards, test results and certificates arrive as they are
   published.
   • Messaging & meetings: direct chat with teachers and the school; book meetings from
   teacher-published slots; announcements feed.
   • Home support: "tonight, ask your child about osmosis" — conversation starters matched to
   this week’s syllabus, so parents help without knowing the content.
   • Language & access: plain-language summaries in English and major Zambian languages;
   (later) USSD result checks for parents without smartphones.



 6. The School World — in Detail
   • Branded account: upload logo, set colors, school name and details — branding flows to the
   dashboard, all generated documents, report cards, certificates and parent-facing views. The
   platform looks like the school.
   • Codes & onboarding: school code; staff codes for teachers; class codes for learners; learner
   codes for guardians — four codes connect an entire school in one afternoon.
   • Oversight dashboard: every teacher, class, grade and subject — planning activity, mastery
   heatmaps, comparative trends, engagement. Weak-competence view across the whole school.
   • Enrollment management: learners, classes, staff directory and roles, guardian-link status —
   one source of truth, no spreadsheets.
   • Announcements & events: targeted broadcasts (one grade, whole school, parents only);
   events calendar (exams, open days, sports).
   • Report cards & certificates: generate the whole term’s reports at once — school-branded
   PDFs; certificates for achievements.
   • Academic analytics: which competences the school struggles with per grade and subject;
   intervention tracking; evidence ready for the Ministry and inspectors.
   • Resource library: school policies, circulars, schemes archive — searchable, always current.
   • Subscriptions & tiers: per-school plans (learners/parents free; schools pay for management
   depth and branding).
   • District & Ministry rollup (later): anonymized aggregates give the Ministry a live national
   picture it has never had.

 8. Study Pathways — the Product Philosophy
   • A pathway is the learner’s continuous route through the curriculum: where they are, what’s
   next, what’s weak, where it leads (careers).
   • It never resets: terms roll over, weak competences resurface for review, new topics connect
   to prior ones automatically.
   • It lives everywhere: every page advances it; offline time counts; audio revision counts; a
   teacher’s lesson moves it; a parent’s evening question reinforces it.
   • Study pathways, not study apps — the difference between a tool and a journey.



 9. Trust, Integrity & Anti-Cheat
   • Permissions first at install (focus/monitoring) — explicit, revocable, parent-controlled. Nothing
   covert, ever.
   • Marking guides separate from questions; revealed only after submission; questions in live
   assignments locked until due date; every solution view logged and auditable by teachers.
   • Per-learner randomized variants defeat answer-key sharing; high-stakes prep uses fresh
   AI-written parallel questions.
   • Database-level security: guardians see only their children; vision processing on-device; no
   raw images stored or transmitted.

 Build order
   • Wks 1–4: schema live; first syllabus digitized; first perfect lesson plan (matches the MoE
   format exactly).
   • Wks 5–8: class codes + scores + adaptive engine; paper pipeline on real ECZ PDFs; school
   branding (logo → documents).
   • Wks 9–12: learner core + Family Link + mocks + focus shield; pilot in 2 schools, one per level
   band.

 Pilot success criteria
   • Teachers save ≥3 planning hours/week; ≥70% weekly active use for a full term.
   • ≥1 guardian linked per learner, viewing weekly; parents rate visibility better than any
   previous channel.
   • Schools use branded documents in real meetings/inspections; ≥3 years of papers per subject
   in the bank.
