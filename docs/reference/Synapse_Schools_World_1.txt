 3. Section One — Command (The Live Dashboard)
   • Today at a glance: attendance so far, lessons in progress, assignments due, announcements
   pending approval.
   • School mastery map: the whole syllabus, every grade — green, amber, red at competence
   level. One screen answers "how are we doing?" — zoom from school → grade → class → learner.
   • Teacher pulse: planning activity, score-entry timeliness, class momentum — support the
   struggling teacher before it shows in results.
   • Guardian coverage: what percentage of learners have a linked parent — and one-tap codes to
   close the gap.
   • Alerts: anomalies surface themselves — a class falling behind pace, a subject
   underperforming across grades, an unmarked backlog.


 4. Section Two — People
   • Roles for everyone: head teacher, deputies, heads of department, teachers, bursar, support
   staff — each with exactly the access they need, nothing more.
   • Four codes onboard a school: school code → staff codes → class codes → learner codes. An
   entire school connected in one afternoon, no IT department required.
   • Staff directory: who teaches what, class loads, contact — always current because it updates
   itself.
   • Enrollment management: learners, class lists, stream moves, new admissions — one source of
   truth, no spreadsheets.
   • Guardian registry: every linked parent, per learner, per class — with connection status and
   history.


 5. Section Three — Academics
   • Syllabus pacing tracker: every class’s position against the scheme of work — who is on pace,
   who is behind, by how many weeks.
   • Exam scheduling & records: the term’s exam calendar with conflict warnings; every result
   archived and analyzable.
   • Intervention tracking: mark a weak competence school-wide, assign interventions, and watch
   the heatmap respond.
   • Moderation: compare marking standards across teachers on the same competence —
   consistency, not blame.
   • Knowledge bank growth: every uploaded paper enriches the whole school’s question bank —
   the school’s library of past papers becomes structured, searchable, exam-generating.

 6. Section Four — Documents & Branding
   • Brand once, everywhere: upload the logo, set colors and details — every plan, scheme, test,
   report card, certificate, transcript and letter carries the school’s identity automatically.
   • Report cards in bulk: the whole term’s reports generated from live mastery data —
   school-branded PDFs, ready for the printing room.
   • AI letter writer: official letters drafted in seconds — absence follow-ups, disciplinary,
   invitations, memos — on the school letterhead, in the right tone, reviewed and sent.
   • Certificates & transcripts: achievements, leavers’ transcripts, competition awards — produced
   in moments, archived forever.
   • Policy center: school policies and circulars versioned and acknowledged — who has read
   what, proven.


 7. Section Five — Communication
   • Targeted announcements: to one grade, one class, staff only, parents only — or the whole
   school; read receipts included.
   • Events calendar: exams, open days, sports, PTA — pushed to the right audiences
   automatically.
   • Emergency broadcast: urgent alerts to all guardians instantly — with delivery confirmation.
   (SMS fallback for non-smartphone parents.)
   • Every channel official: because conversations live in Synapse, the school has a record — no
   more "the WhatsApp group said" disputes.


 8. Section Six — Analytics (Evidence)
   • Any cut of the data: by teacher, class, grade, subject, gender, term, year — a question away,
   not a week of spreadsheet work.
   • Year-over-year trends: is the school improving? Where? Since when? What changed?
   • Benchmarking: compare against anonymized schools of similar profile — context for targets.
   • Forecasting: predicted exam outcomes from current mastery — act months early, not after
   results.
   • Inspection & Ministry exports: clean, branded, evidence-grade reports generated on demand
   — the inspection meeting starts from strength.

 12. Trust, Privacy & Governance
   • Role-based access down to the row level: a teacher sees their classes; a deputy sees their
   grade; the head sees the school; the Ministry sees anonymized aggregates — and nothing
   more.
   • Learners are minors: guardian visibility is per-child only, monitoring is opt-in and
   parent-controlled, sensitive processing stays on-device.
   • Every action logged: who saw what, who changed what — governance built in, not bolted on.
   • The school owns its data: full export at any time, no lock-in hostage-taking. Confidence,
   stated in writing.



 13. First Build (MVP Slice for Schools)
   • Command dashboard, People (staff/learners/guardians + codes), branded documents and
   bulk report cards, targeted announcements, core analytics and Ministry export.
   • Later phases: operations modules (timetable, fees), benchmarking and forecasting, SMS
   fallback, multi-school groups.



 14. Why Schools Will Love It
   • Zero adoption friction: the network is already there — the school simply claims it, brands it,
   and switches on the console.
   • Identity restored: every document that leaves the school carries its name — pride in print,
   credibility in inspection.
   • Evidence instead of anecdotes: staff meetings, board meetings, Ministry meetings — all start
   from live data.
   • Money back to the mission: one subscription replaces the patchwork, and teacher hours
   return to teaching.
   • Synapse doesn’t ask the school to change how it works. It asks the school to stop paying for
   five tools when one nervous system already connects everyone it serves.
