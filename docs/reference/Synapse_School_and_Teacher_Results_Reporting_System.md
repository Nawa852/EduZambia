# Synapse: School & Teacher Results Reporting & Document Engine

**Subsystems:** School Administrator Console & Teacher Classroom Command Center  
**Compliance Standard:** Ministry of Education (MoE) Zambia & Examinations Council of Zambia (ECZ / CBC 2023)  
**Document Architecture:** Automated Server-Side & Local Client PDF Generation Engine  
**Target:** Synapse Learning Hub (`learn-smart-zambia-hub`)  

---

## 1. Executive Architecture Overview

Synapse unifies academic results reporting, lesson planning, and institutional oversight into an automated, single-source document pipeline. Instead of teachers manually formatting Word documents or admins compiling scattered Excel spreadsheets:

1. **Teachers** log daily class competencies, quizzes, and continuous assessments (CA).
2. **The Synapse Intelligence Engine** aggregates scores, maps them against official CDC learning outcomes, and drafts personalized qualitative comments.
3. **School Admins** review school-wide results, verify syllabus pacing compliance, apply institutional branding, and generate bulk inspection-ready PDF outputs in one click.

## 2. The Teacher’s World: Planning & Results Reporting

### 2.1 Ministry of Education 5-Stage Lesson Plan Specification

Every lesson plan generated by Synapse strictly adheres to the Directorate of Curriculum Development (CDC) inspection format:

* **Header Metadata:** School Name, EMIS Number, Teacher Name, Grade/Form, Subject, Date, Duration (e.g., 80 mins double period), Class Size.
* **Curriculum Alignment:** Framework (ZECF 2023 / CBC), General Competence, Specific Learning Outcome Code, Lesson Rationale, Prior Knowledge.
* **Resources & Materials:** Realistic, low-resource teaching aids (e.g., Manila paper, local materials, standard textbooks).
* **5-Stage Progression Matrix:**

| Stage | Time | Teacher Activity | Learner Activity | Core Competencies & Skills | Success Criteria / Assessment |
|:---|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. Introduction** | 10 min | Recalls prior knowledge via rapid diagnostic questions; presents real-world scenario. | Answers prompts, discusses baseline problem in pairs. | Critical thinking, active listening. | 80% learners correctly state prior concept. |
| **2. Lesson Development** | 35 min | Step-by-step concept modeling, guided inquiry, worked examples on board. | Takes notes, works collaborative sample problems, asks clarifying questions. | Problem solving, analytical reasoning, collaboration. | Step-by-step procedure accurately demonstrated. |
| **3. Exercise / Assessment** | 20 min | Distributes structured exercise; circulates to provide differentiated support. | Individual execution of 3 progressive difficulty questions. | Independent application, numeracy/literacy. | Minimum 70% threshold achieved on practice set. |
| **4. Homework / Extension** | 5 min | Assigns reinforcement task and higher-order challenge problem. | Records assignment into digital or paper notebook. | Self-directed inquiry, retention. | Homework task noted with clear rubric. |
| **5. Conclusion & Review** | 10 min | Facilitates 3-minute synthesis; asks random learners to summarize core learning. | Articulates lesson takeaway; completes 1-minute exit ticket. | Communication, meta-cognition. | Exit ticket confirms key concept mastered. |

### 2.2 Teacher Gradebook & Results Logging

* **Formative Continuous Assessment (CA):** Teachers record competence mastery directly from quizzes, class exercises, and practicals.
* **Mastery Thresholds:**
  * **Exceeding Standard (85%–100% / Grade 1 / Distinction):** Demonstrates complete conceptual mastery and novel problem solving.
  * **Meeting Standard (65%–84% / Grade 2–3 / Merit):** Consistently achieves specific learning outcomes without assistance.
  * **Approaching Standard (50%–64% / Grade 4–6 / Credit):** Grasps foundational concepts but requires guided scaffolding.
  * **Below Standard (<50% / Needs Urgent Intervention):** Targeted for automated adaptive warm-ups and remedial support.
* **AI-Assisted Report Comments:** The engine auto-generates accurate, empathetic teacher remarks grounded in performance data (e.g., *"Mwansa has shown outstanding command of Algebraic Expressions (Code: CBC-SEC-M1.2), though additional focus is recommended on Geometric Proofs"*), which the teacher can edit with a single tap.

## 3. The School Admin Console: Results & Document Command

### 3.1 School Identity & Branding Core (`/school/settings`)

All generated documents automatically inherit the school's official identity configured by the Head Teacher:
* **School Crest / Emblem:** High-resolution digital insignia embedded as vector/base64 asset.
* **Color Palette:** Primary institutional color (applied to headers and border highlights) and secondary accent.
* **Official Header Block:** School Name, Ministry of Education Center Code, EMIS Number, District, Postal Address, Official Contact Email & Phone.
* **Official Motto:** Displayed prominently on the document footer.

### 3.3 School-Wide Results & Academic Intervention Ledger

School Admins and Heads of Department (HoD) access the aggregated term ledger:
1. **Pass Rate & Merit Distribution:** Percentage of students scoring Distinction (Grades 1–2), Merit (3–4), Credit (5–6), Pass (7–8), and Unsatisfactory (9).
2. **Subject Performance Variance:** Identifies which departments or classes are underperforming relative to the school average.
3. **Syllabus Pacing Compliance:** Compares each teacher's actual delivered topics against the termly CDC scheme of work, flagging classes that are 2+ weeks behind schedule before mock exams begin.
4. **Intervention Tracking:** Enables the Head Teacher to assign targeted remediation programs to students flagging red in critical subjects (e.g., Mathematics and English).

## 4. Technical Implementation & PDF Generation Pipeline

In Synapse, documents are generated using a dual-engine strategy:

1. **Client-Side Rapid Generation (`@react-pdf/renderer` or `window.print()` CSS Paged Media):**
   - High-fidelity print styles (`@media print`) allow teachers and admins to print or save PDFs instantly from their web browser with zero server latency.
   - Page margins are fixed at standard A4 dimensions (`210mm x 297mm`) with explicit page-break management (`page-break-inside: avoid`).

2. **Automated Batch Worker Pipeline:**
   - When generating an entire grade's report cards (e.g., 200 learners in Grade 11), a background queue compiles individual student records, renders the branded templates, and combines them into an archival ZIP or single combined PDF for the school's print room.
   - Every generated report card includes a cryptographically secure verification QR code linking to `/verify/[report_token]` on Synapse, allowing employers, colleges, or scholarship boards to verify authentic results without phone calls.
