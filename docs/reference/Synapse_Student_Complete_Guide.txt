6. THE UNIFIED AI HUB
Stop making students hunt through 15 screens. One input. One button. Six outputs.


The New Flow:
   1. Student types or uploads anything — photo of the board, PDF, pasted notes, or just a topic like

   2. They hit one button: 'Synapse It — Break It All Down.'

   3. AI generates everything at once: Key Points, Flashcards, ECZ Quiz, Visual Lesson, Video Picks,
   Study Buddies.

8. THE ZAMBIA / ECZ CONTEXT
Your user profile shows Grade 10, Western Province, Zambia. This is not Silicon Valley. Data costs,
device storage, and exam pressure are real constraints.


What Works in Zambia:
   Offline Video Cache — Brilliant. Keep it. Data is expensive and connectivity is spotty.

   Lightweight app — Keep the app under 30MB. With 26+ screens, you are likely already bloated.

   ECZ alignment — Not Harvard CS50. A student in Nalionwa needs to pass exams, not learn Python.

   Family Link via WhatsApp — Parents use WhatsApp, not email. Send weekly progress reports as
   WhatsApp-friendly images or short text.


What Burns Data (Use Sparingly):
   Video Rooms — Real-time video burns MBs fast. Zambian students on 4G (look at your screenshots) will
   avoid this.

   Auto-play videos — Never auto-play. Always ask.

   High-res images — Compress all uploaded photos to <500KB before AI processing.


The ECZ Moat:
   YouTube has Harvard CS50. It does not have ECZ-aligned practice quizzes with
   instant scoring for Grade 10 Biology in Western Province. Own the exam. Everything
   else is a distraction.
9. FAMILY LINK — YOUR GROWTH HACK
The parent/guardian invite code is smart, but you are underselling it. Parents in Zambia care deeply
about ECZ results. They pay for data. They will share your app with other parents if it proves their child
is studying.


Current Messaging (Weak):
  "Linked guardians can see attendance, grades and homework progress — never your private notes,
  chats or study material."


Better Messaging (Strong):
   "Get weekly ECZ quiz scores and study time sent straight to your WhatsApp. Know
   exactly how your child is preparing for exams."


Action Items:
   1. One-tap WhatsApp share for invite codes (not 'generate code and hope they type it in').

   2. Weekly automated report: quiz scores, focus time, flashcards reviewed, streak count.

   3. Parent dashboard is read-only and mobile-optimized. No complex navigation.

   4. Celebrate milestones: 'Nawa completed 5 ECZ quizzes this week!' → shareable to family group.

  → Parents are your distribution. Make them evangelists with weekly progress reports.

  → Zambia first, world later. ECZ alignment > Harvard CS50 until you own the local market.

  → Your code is safe. Hide features with flags. Do not delete. Re-enable when you have density.

  → Test on real Zambian syllabus content. AI hallucination on a past paper is a trust-killer.
